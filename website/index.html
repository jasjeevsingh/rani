Everything You Need for Research

Built by researchers, for researchers. RANI combines powerful AI with privacy-first design.

Context-Aware AI

RANI sees what you're reading on screen and hears what you're discussing. Ask questions with full context—no more copy-pasting excerpts.

  • Screen capture integration
  • Real-time audio transcription
  • Conversation memory across sessions

Smart Document Library

Upload, organize, and annotate PDFs with AI-powered semantic search. Find connections across your entire research library.

  • PDF import & annotation
  • Vector embeddings for semantic search
  • Cross-document reference linking

Paper Discovery

Search arXiv and Semantic Scholar directly from your desktop. Import papers with metadata and PDFs automatically.

  • arXiv API integration
  • Semantic Scholar search (coming soon)
  • Automatic citation tracking

Meeting Assistant

Record and transcribe meetings, lectures, and interviews in real-time. Get AI-generated summaries and action items.

  • Live transcription (mic + system audio)
  • Speaker diarization
  • Auto-generated meeting notes

Multi-Provider AI

Choose from OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or run models locally with Ollama. No vendor lock-in.

  • Bring your own API keys
  • Local models (privacy-first)
  • Research-tuned prompts

Privacy First

Your research data stays on your device with the free tier. Choose your privacy level with optional cloud features.

  • On-device AI models available
  • Flexible privacy options
  • Secure data handling
