Everything You Need for Research

Built for researchers, by researchers. RANI combines powerful AI with privacy-first design.

Context-Aware AI

RANI sees what you're reading on screen and hears what you're discussing. Ask questions with full context—no more copy-pasting excerpts.

  • Screen capture integration
  • Real-time audio transcription
  • Conversation memory across sessions

Smart Document Library

Upload, organize, and annotate PDFs with AI-powered semantic search. Always receive scientifically accurate suggestions. No more hallucinations.

  • PDF import & annotation
  • Vector embeddings for semantic search
  • Cross-document reference linking

Paper Discovery

Search arXiv directly from your desktop. Import papers with metadata and PDFs automatically.

  • arXiv API integration
  • Connection to Zotero
  • Automatic citation tracking (coming soon)

Meeting Assistant

Record and transcribe meetings, lectures, and interviews in real-time. Get AI-generated summaries and action items.

  • Live transcription (mic + system audio)
  • Speaker diarization
  • Auto-generated meeting notes

Multi-Provider AI

Choose from OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, or run models locally with Ollama. No vendor lock-in.

  • Local LLM models
  • Local embedding models
  • Designed for assistance, not summarization

Privacy First

Your research data stays on your device when in private mode. Cloud features are optional.

  • Option for completely on-device computations
  • Flexible privacy options
  • Secure data handling

Your Research, Your Data

RANI is built with privacy at its core. Your documents, conversations, and research data stay on your device. No cloud storage, no data mining, no third-party access.

When you use cloud AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic), only the specific content you query is sent—never your entire library. Our free tier uses 100% local processing with Ollama.
